# Shipping.com

Shipping.com is a shipping intelligence and marketplace platform that provides inside information on the best deals across the shipping industry worldwide. The platform enables carriers, shippers, and third-party logistics companies (3PLs) to access reduced shipping rates, improve revenue opportunities, and optimize transportation supply chain operations. Shipping.com is headquartered in New York City and connects members of the transportation and logistics industry with competitive pricing and market intelligence.

**Human URL:** [https://www.shipping.com/](https://www.shipping.com/)

## Overview

- Shipping rate intelligence and comparisons
- Freight marketplace connecting carriers and shippers
- Transportation procurement tools
- Market data and analytics for logistics professionals
